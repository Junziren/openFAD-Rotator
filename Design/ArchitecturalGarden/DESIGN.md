# Acoustic Architecture

An instrument becomes a small civic landscape. A central mechanism supplies
the identity; four structures supply the vocabulary. Their silhouettes must
remain recognizable without material, motion, or explanatory prose. The
composition is meticulously crafted around usable negative space rather than
ornamental complexity.

Chalk, porcelain, cool stone, and restrained enamel describe mass without
imitating aged hardware. Warm coral, mineral green, blue-green, and pale
yellow distinguish families, while graphite belongs to the mechanism.
Painstaking attention to edge thickness and contact shadows makes modest
polygon counts feel intentional.

Architecture is grounded. A stair reaches a landing; a column meets its roof;
a moving slab has a telescoping support. Empty openings matter as much as
solid material. No impossible paths, floating scenery, atmospheric particles,
or copied game landmarks are necessary.

Scale establishes the hierarchy: the cutaway rotary speaker is the tallest
and most detailed object, buildings are secondary, and landscaping is quiet.
The orthographic composition is calibrated at both plugin sizes. This is a
study in careful craft, not a claim of photorealistic hardware manufacture.

Movement preserves identity. The tower extends, the pavilion lifts, the
bridge opens, and the terraces rise. Fixed footprints and stable label zones
make change understandable. The same precision applies to the hidden work:
named pivots, closed shells, reproducible exports, and verifiable state poses.

## Authority and scope

- Primary: the approved stage-one brief in this thread.
- Secondary: FAD/Unpure Bloom neutral instrument chrome and the actual
  openFAD Rotator macro IDs.
- Observed: an existing React/WebGL2/JUCE plugin; Blender 5.2.2 LTS available.
- Preserved: all plugin code, DSP, parameter/state ABI, presets and packaging.
- Rejected: copied game scenes, trademarked cabinet styling, runtime coupling,
  fake telemetry, glow, particles, and an editable scene-construction tool.
- This asset study is not an implemented plugin UI or a measured Leslie model.
